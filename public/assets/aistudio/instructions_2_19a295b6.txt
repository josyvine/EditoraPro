- qa/VisualQaManager.kt: Orchestrator executing the two-step Blind Visual Audit and Code Rewrite self-healing loop.
- export/VideoExporter.kt: Deterministic frame-by-frame hardware video encoder and muxer outputting exact-duration MP4 files.
